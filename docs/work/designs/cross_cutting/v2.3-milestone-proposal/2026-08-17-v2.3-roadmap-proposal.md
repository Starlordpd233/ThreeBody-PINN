### Three findings that changed the draft

**Finding 1 — the draft's Option A had no artifact behind it.** The 2026-08-17
draft offered "fill trainex-r2's existing Q3 decision sheet directly" as the
fast path. **No such decision sheet exists.** The strings `decision sheet`,
`suggested default`, `one-way`/`two-way`, and `door type` appear nowhere in the
trainex-r2 report, its `RESEARCH-CONTEXT-MASTER.md`, or its
`RESEARCH-AGENT-PROMPT.md`. This is by design, not oversight: that round's
output contract (master §7, clause 3) reads **"Do not recommend a remedy."**
trainex-r2 was commissioned under SCOPE-26 as a *diagnosis-framing* round and
was deliberately stripped of the options-menu / decision-sheet contract the ERW
runbook's Rule 7 requires of build-design rounds.

v2.2 has since consumed most of what Q3 *did* deliver:

| trainex-r2 Q3 content | Status after v2.2 |
|---|---|
| Reading 2 — frame inconsistency inflates the residual floor | **Real; repaired.** Became C-09/CONF-09; the residual frame is audited conformant (2026-08-17) |
| Reading 1 — `ω₀=30` SIREN second derivative | **Confirmed**, no longer a hypothesis |
| Discriminating test 1 — residual-on-stored-targets | **Executed** (CONF-01 / CONF-08 / CONF-09) |
| Discriminating test 3 — per-term gradient statistics | **Executed** (INST-02; median `∇phys/∇data` = 6.3×10¹²) |
| Discriminating test 2 — perturbation response curve | not done |
| Discriminating test 4 — λ ablation | now barred as tuning (SCOPE-37) |

What remains open is precisely the remedy — the one thing Q3 was contractually
forbidden to touch ("this report only maps it"). Option A as drafted would have
required *someone* to author the options menu; under SCOPE-34 and the repo's
STOP protocol that someone cannot be the agent. Hence decision 1's staged form:
the packet is assembled and framed, never recommended, and the owner gate decides
whether it is sufficient to rule on or must go out as `trainex-r3`.

**Finding 2 — `η_c = 1` is a locked owner-ratified contract, not a default.**
`pinn_architecture.md:424` carries a Phase-35 contract block: **LOSS-04 /
D-04 RESOLVED-WITH-INSTRUCTION / owner-ratified Option A / AC-LOSS-04**,
grounded in the v1.6 resolution doc §1.1 and GAP-32-01. SPEC-01 is therefore a
formal amendment to a v1.6-locked adjudicated decision that carries an
acceptance criterion — not a parameter edit. Two adjacent locks matter for the
same reason: the same contract block states the causal-exponent clip is
*"required only to avoid underflow"* (so raising the clip floor as a remedy
changes the clip's declared purpose), and `first_omega_0` is *"fixed at `30`
and is not scheduled during training"* (`pinn_architecture.md:167`). New scope
boundary **SCOPE-38** governs this.

**Finding 3 — `η_c` is coupled to `N_t`, and `N_t` is currently spec-divergent.**
`pinn/loss_catalog.py` computes the causal weight from an **exclusive prefix
cumulative sum** of `R̄`, so the exponent at point *n* grows with the number of
preceding collocation points. The code runs `collocation_count = 16`
(`pinn/train.py:1033`); the spec specifies `n_col = 4096`, "recommended:
2048–4096" (`pinn_architecture.md:433,440`). Calibrating `η_c` at 16 and later
honoring 4096 moves the exponent by roughly 256× — the identical "two locked
choices never checked against each other" failure, one layer down, inside the
very section being edited. v2.2 dispositioned `n_col` as a deferred recipe
surface under SCOPE-29; that deferral expires here. Hence decision 2 and
requirement SPEC-06.

## Why this milestone exists

v2.2 closed incomplete on 2026-08-17. It repaired all nine verified Stage-0
conformance divergences and re-ran Stage-0 under the corrected recipe — the
first run that was actually the specified experiment. That run HALTed at the
invariant-B causal-front gate, step 501, `front_idx=0` for the entire `N=500`
window. A bounded residual-frame conformance audit the same day established
this is **not** a further conformance defect: it is a design-calibration gap
between two independently-locked framework-spec sections. The locked SIREN
initialization (`pinn_architecture.md` §2.2, `ω₀=30`) produces a second
derivative of `O(10⁵)` at initialization; the locked causal-weighting formula
(§3.2, `w_n = exp(−η_c·Σ R̄_m)`, `η_c=1` on unnormalized `R̄`) was calibrated
for a converged network's residual of `O(1)`. Neither choice is wrong in
isolation — they were simply never checked against each other — but stacked,
the causal gate is saturated shut from step 0 and stays saturated: 15 of 16
collocation-point weights sit at the numerical floor for the entire run.

This was exactly the contingency v2.2's own goal statement named in advance:
*"If the conformant run still fails, that failure is the first real evidence
about the design, and recipe adjudication... is v2.3."* v2.3 is that
milestone.

## Milestone goal

Get a properly grounded recalibration decision for the causal-weight/SIREN-scale
mismatch — assembled and framed by the agent, ruled on by the owner, and
externally researched via the ERW build-design channel if the assembled packet
does not support a ruling — implement it as a framework-spec **contract
amendment** (finally legal here; SCOPE-30 does not carry forward), prove the fix
train-free before spending a run, re-run Stage-0 under the corrected recipe, and
spend the single preserved held-out evaluation on a run that actually trains.

**What this milestone does NOT do:** re-litigate the nine v2.2 conformance
repairs (settled, not reopened), re-run DIAG-05 (superseded, not
re-litigated — see the re-derivation), or open Stage-1+/PLAT-01..07 (still
blocked by the v2.0 `adjust` verdict until a `go` verdict is rendered here).

**Declared terminal state.** If GATE-04 refuses the recalibration train-free, or
RUN-04's causal front still fails to advance under the ratified recipe, SCOPE-37
forbids further tuning without a new owner decision. The milestone then closes on
a `rethink`-flavored DIAG-06 verdict with the held-out evaluation preserved
unspent — declared here up front, so that outcome is a clean close rather than a
drift. v2.2's lesson: the terminal branch must be named before it is needed.

## ERW approach — RATIFIED: staged (packet, then gate)

Decision 1 above. In sequence:

1. **ERW-01 assembles a strictly non-recommending decision packet**: the
   confirmed mechanism; the coupled remedy surfaces (`η_c`, `R̄` normalization,
   the SIREN init scale `ω₀`, `N_t`/`n_col`, and the clip's declared semantics);
   what v2.2's evidence has already settled from trainex-r2 Q3 (Finding 1's
   table); and an explicit statement of what is *not* in hand. No option is
   recommended, no default marked, no ranking implied.
2. **Owner gate.** Reading the assembled packet, the owner either rules directly
   or commissions `trainex-r3`.
3. **ERW-02 discharges whichever branch fires.** If `trainex-r3` is
   commissioned, it is a Route-B (open-book) build-design round against the
   post-repair tree, carrying the full ERW Rule-7 output contract that
   trainex-r2 deliberately lacked — 2–4 option menus, suggested defaults marked,
   one-way vs two-way door classification, and a fill-in decision sheet — plus
   the mandatory Rule-19 per-claim in-repo verification pass.

The packet is the `trainex-r3` `RESEARCH-CONTEXT-MASTER.md` either way, so
neither branch wastes work. This answers the 2026-08-17 audit's cost objection
("what is missing is an owner disposition, not more literature") without
assuming the disposition is available before the picture is assembled.

## Risks

- **The recalibration might not be a single clean parameter change.** Trainex-r2's
  Q3 already flags that `R̄` normalization interacts with the causal front's
  *within-trajectory* curriculum semantics — fixing the saturation without
  breaking the intended pacing needs care, likely more than one iteration inside
  the decision. SPEC-06 adds a second coupled axis (`N_t`).
- **A second causal-front failure under the new recipe is possible.** SCOPE-37's
  conformance-only-after-the-edit discipline exists precisely so a second failure
  is informative (a real recipe-adjudication miss) rather than another
  conformance leak. GATE-04 moves most of that risk earlier and makes it cheap.
- **Trainex-r2 is 2026-07-25-vintage** — pinned to a commit baseline before the
  v2.2 conformance repairs landed, and written under a "do not recommend a
  remedy" contract. This is why the ERW gate exists rather than a direct
  transcription of its Q3.
- **SCOPE-38 raises the bar on the spec edit.** Amending three v1.6-locked
  contracts with re-issued acceptance criteria is more work than editing three
  numbers. That cost is the point: the mismatch v2.3 exists to fix was produced
  by two locked choices that were never checked against each other.
