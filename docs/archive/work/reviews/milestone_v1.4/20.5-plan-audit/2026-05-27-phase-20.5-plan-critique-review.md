- `depends_on` frontmatter across Phases 16–19 (GSD dependency convention)
- Repo-wide reference scan for `GAP-20-5-*` / `20-5-*` (rename/normalization footprint)

## Headline verdict

**The critique is partially correct.** Its two "Critical" gaps (1.1, 1.2) are **real and well-caught in substance**, but their proposed resolutions are **incomplete**; one parity item (2.3) is a **likely false positive** rooted in a GSD-model misunderstanding; one (2.1) is **over-confident** and touches locked records; and the critique **misses two HIGH-severity defects** (M-1, M-2) that are at least as consequential as anything it found. It should **not** be adopted verbatim.

## Part 2 — Unstated assumptions in the critique

Verifying the critique's *premises* (not just its conclusions) surfaced two things it got wrong or didn't notice:

- **Symlink topology.** The critique treats `CLAUDE.md` / `GEMINI.md` / `AGENTS.md` / `ANTIGRAVITY.md` as four independent files. In reality `CLAUDE.md` and `GEMINI.md` are **symlinks → `AGENTS.md`**, and `ANTIGRAVITY.md` is a **separate 15 KB file** carrying its own copy of the `pinn/` directive (`ANTIGRAVITY.md:196`). The critique's "add `AGENTS.md`" resolution therefore **silently misses `ANTIGRAVITY.md`**, which was an explicit monolithic Task-3 target.
- **`VER-05` is valid (not a defect).** The split-plan frontmatter references `VER-05`, which looks like it might be a dangling requirement. It is **real and applies milestone-wide** (`REQUIREMENTS.md:59`, status "Complete", "Phase 16 (defined) / Phases 17–21 (applied)"). This is *not* an issue — recorded here so it isn't re-flagged later.
