<objective>
Survey every stage of the training data read-only, with the predictability envelope,
and select the two hard-stage probe systems by their frozen rule (FEAS-08).

In plain language: before anything is trained beyond Stage 0, we look at all of the
later stages to see how hard each one is: how fast the inner pair spins, how close
bodies come, how violent the accelerations are, and how quickly a small error grows
(the Lyapunov time). That tells the owner whether a Stage-0 success could hide a dead
end later. The survey also picks the two systems Phase 60 will probe.

Purpose: D-18 (as revised by D-33), D-19, D-20, D-21, D-40 (training-split records only) and D-41 (the long pass runs detached with a memory preflight and a completion marker). The survey is diagnostic: it
reports and never gates, and its stage labels are not the official curriculum mapping,
which stays owner-pending.

Output: pinn/verification/dataset_survey.py, its tests, and the survey evidence area.
</objective>
