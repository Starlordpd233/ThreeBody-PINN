<objective>
Run the ruling's first read-only checkpoint experiments on generation 7's retained
checkpoints, against generation 7's own code: JX-10 (verification completion) and the
executed half of JX-1 (per-term gradient decomposition and Adam-preconditioned shares),
and record Gate G1 (DIAG-07).

In plain language: generation 7's saved checkpoints are re-examined without training
them. First we check that re-running the run's own "is the front advancing" monitor
reproduces the numbers recorded at the time. Then we measure how much each part of the
loss pushed the network at steps 0, 900 and 951, and what share of the actual Adam
update the physics term got. If physics got at least 1% of the update, Gate G1 fires
and the plan for Phase 61 is re-examined before Phase 61 opens.

Purpose: D-22 as revised by D-29, D-23 as refined by D-36, D-24, D-25, D-27, D-34,
and D-41 (DIAG-07 is not a SCOPE-44 training run, but its long steps run detached
with a memory preflight and a completion marker). The ruling authorizes forward and
backward passes on retained checkpoints; no optimizer step is taken.

Output: the DIAG-07 driver (common library and the JX-1/JX-10 script), its tests, and
the jx10, jx01 and g1 evidence.
</objective>
