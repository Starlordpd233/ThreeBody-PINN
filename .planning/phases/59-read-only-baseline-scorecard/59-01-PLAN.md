<objective>
Freeze the feasibility scorecard before anything is measured (FEAS-01, SCOPE-42).

In plain language: this plan writes down the ruler and every rule the rest of the
phase and milestone will be judged by, and locks it with a fingerprint (SHA-256),
before a single number is computed on real data. That makes it impossible to move a
pass mark after seeing a result without the change showing up.

Purpose: SCOPE-42 pre-registration. Every census, baseline, survey and DIAG-07 result in
Waves 2-5 is computed after this plan's commits; `verify_phase59_evidence.py
scorecard-frozen` (Plan 59-04) checks the commit order mechanically.

Output: the machine-readable scorecard, its strict loader and rule functions, their
tests, the plain-language scorecard document, and the freeze record.
</objective>
