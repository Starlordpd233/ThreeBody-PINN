<objective>
Measure how hard the Stage-0 target is, prove the data path matches the trainer's, and
choose the panels by the frozen rule (FEAS-02).

In plain language: for each of the 1,812 Stage-0 training systems we count how many
times the inner pair goes around in the window, and how precisely a network would have
to time those laps to stay within 1% or 10% at the end. Before that, we prove our
reader sees exactly the same numbers the trainer saw. Then the frozen rule picks the
three single systems, the 16-system family and the validation panel that every later
experiment uses.

Purpose: D-12, D-13, D-31, D-32, D-35 and D-40 (the census proper reads training records only; validation records are read only for the agreement check and the validation panel; the test split is never read). The panels and the phase-accuracy figure are
inputs to Phase 60, Plan 59-08 (comparison points) and Plan 59-09 (JX-4 fixtures).

Output: pinn/verification/stage0_census.py, its tests, and the audit and census
evidence areas.
</objective>
