**Background:** Line 287 in Table 1 ("Architectural hyperparameters of the SQR-SkipResNet-SIREN backbone") shows `U[-hidden_omega_0/(6·n_in), hidden_omega_0/(6·n_in)]`, which is **not** the SIREN initialization. SI-PINN-001 explicitly designates the `√(6/n_in)/hidden_omega_0` form as canonical; this task aligns Table 1 with that designation.
