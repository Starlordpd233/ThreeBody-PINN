## 3. Citation guide (for Phase 25 evidence packets)

EVID-06 requires line-range citations into the candidate verdict source. Conventions:

- **File path (verbatim):**
  `pinn_instruction/markdown_conversion/framework-revisions/research_reports/02-data-pipeline-and-validation/02-resolution-doc.md`
- **Section anchor:** cite by the doc's own §-number and Q-ID, e.g. `§4.4 Q-B4` or `§5.1 Q-C1`.
- **Line-range format:** `02-resolution-doc.md:493–536` (1-based, inclusive). Line numbers in this map
  are anchored to the committed 820-line revision (2026-06-11, 157,460 bytes). Re-verify line numbers
  if the doc is re-delivered after a fix; section/Q-ID anchors are stable across minor re-pagination.
- **Verdict clause:** within a question subsection, cite the numbered part (e.g. "§4.4 part 1, clause 3"
  for the E_scale = 1e-8 canonicalization), since each subsection follows the fixed 7-part structure.
- **Proposed spec text:** each question's part 6 carries the proposed `ctl_instruction.md` /
  `pinn_architecture.md` edit; Phases 26–28 consume these as candidate edits, not as authority.
- **Authority reminder for packets:** the doc is a candidate verdict source. Phase 25 verdicts must
  cite the empirical grounding (24-VERIFIED-FACTS.md CLM rows, framework-spec lines) alongside the
  doc's recommendation — the doc's agreement is not itself the verdict.
