<planner_assumptions>
## Edge R4 (Huber transition + causal-weight extremes) — planner assumption

At `|x| = delta` the Huber loss switches between its quadratic and linear branches; at the causal-weight extreme `w_n -> 0` (large accumulated upstream residual) a time point's physics contribution vanishes. Handling (surfaced per 38-SPEC Edge Coverage, not a held-out obligation): (a) term-level tests exercise inputs straddling `|x| = delta` and confirm continuity of value and first derivative at the transition (torch's Huber is C1 at the knot); (b) the causal weight uses `stopgrad` on the accumulated residual (so the weight cannot become an adversarial term) and clips the exponent only to avoid underflow — no numeric clip bound is invented here (the spec defers the bound to FUT); tests confirm `w_n` stays finite and in (0, 1] as the upstream residual grows. This is a planner assumption, not a held-out test obligation.
</planner_assumptions>
