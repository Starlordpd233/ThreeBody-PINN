Rationale: overfitting one trajectory's data term is a small, fast smoke of the model+COM wiring; a fixed cap makes non-convergence a bounded FAIL, never a hang. Calibration guidance: on a cap-reached FAIL the evidence's achieved ratio distinguishes a still-improving optimization (cap too low → re-run with a larger `--max-steps`) from a plateau (a genuine wiring problem). A one-shot calibration (run once with a generous cap, e.g. 10000, observe the convergence step, set the default to 2-3x that) is recommended and recorded in evidence; the default is NEVER a wiring verdict on its own.
