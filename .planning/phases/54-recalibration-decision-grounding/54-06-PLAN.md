## What this plan is, and what it is not

Two commits landed on `milestone/v2.3` on 2026-08-21 outside the plan ledger:

- `11b4e0c1` — *docs(54): discharge checkpoint one and assemble the trainex-r3 package*
- `ad51968e` — *docs(54): stamp the rule-20 live-tree pin for the trainex-r3 handoff*

They were authored in an interactive session under direct owner instruction.
There was no authorizing plan, no plan-checker pass, and no SUMMARY.

**This plan does not retroactively authorize those commits.** A plan written
after the work is not a plan, and presenting it as an authorization would record
a false sequence in the ledger. What this plan authorizes is the
**reconciliation**: recording what landed and when, retiring the guard the pass
invalidated and installing a replacement, re-deriving by command every audit
result the pass recorded by hand, verifying the packet's own amendment record
against its cited sources, and correcting the phase accounting.

Anyone reading this phase directory later must be able to see exactly what
happened and in what order. That is the deliverable.

**Tracer note.** This phase's tracer is `54-01` (wave 1), which proved the
round-root-plus-bars shape end to end. `54-06` and `54-07` are expansion plans on
that proven shape; no new tracer is emitted.

**ROADMAP note.** The planner deliberately left `.planning/ROADMAP.md` untouched
when authoring this plan. Its Phase 54 block is one of the three documents whose
accounting disagrees, and correcting it is Task 3's deliverable under plan
authority — derived from disk, in one place, inside the ledger. Editing it at
plan-authoring time would repeat the pattern this plan exists to repair.
