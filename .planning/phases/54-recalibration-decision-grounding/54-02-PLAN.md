## The coupling that must be unmissable (D-05, SPEC-06)

`w_n` is an **exclusive** prefix sum over collocation points — `cumsum_excl[0] = 0`,
`cumsum_excl[n] = Σ_{m<n} R̄_m.detach()`, implemented by right-shifting a
`torch.cumsum` and prepending a zero (`pinn/loss_catalog.py:326-342`, with the
shift at `:330-333` and `w₁ = 1` pinned at `:340`). So the exponent at point *n*
grows with the **count** of preceding points. `pinn/train.py:1033` runs
`collocation_count = 16`; `pinn_architecture.md:454` specifies `n_col = 4096`
with `:461` reading "recommended: 2048-4096". `4096 / 16 = 256` exactly, so
calibrating at one count and honoring the other moves the exponent by roughly
**256×**. This is verifiable arithmetic on two already-fixed numbers — stating
it is mechanism, not a recommendation.
