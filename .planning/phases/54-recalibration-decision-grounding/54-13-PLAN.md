<objective>
Prepare the ruling record for the owner's second checkpoint, present the gate,
and stop.

Purpose: the recalibration decision is owner-ratified and never agent-decided.
Everything this pass produced — the verified claims, the adjudicated premises,
the routed gaps, the consolidated candidate document — exists so that the owner
can rule on evidence rather than on assertion. The one thing none of it does is
rule. The consolidated decision sheet is a candidate presented for ratification
and never the ratification itself, and the record that carries the ratification is
this one.

Output: an additive preparation block in `54-RULING.md` — the pointer list, the
per-surface companion table, the restated completeness rule and the framing for
each re-ratification block — plus a blocking owner gate. **Phase 54 does not
close in this pass.** The anchor gap stays open, the second checkpoint stays
unfilled, and the downstream phase stays closed until the owner discharges it.

Per D-09 the ruling record is where a ruling lives and a ruling is not a gap; per
D-08 the checkpoint is a structured record the owner fills rather than a free-text
ask; and per D-18 this record receives pointers and never menus.
</objective>

<planning_provenance>
## Standing

`54-08` through `54-12` discharged the post-delivery obligations the handoff
readiness record named: the returned reports are certified at intake, the
access-mode divergence is recorded with its consequences, the live-tree pin is
stamped, every claim carries a verdict, every cross-report agreement carries an
entitlement, the premises are adjudicated, the surfaced decision points are
routed, and the round's output is consolidated into one candidate document. What
remains is the owner's.

**Tracer note.** This phase's tracer is `54-01`. `54-13` is an expansion plan on
that proven shape; no new tracer is emitted.

**The ruling record becomes writable here, and the transition is declared.** Every
plan of this pass so far asserts `54-RULING.md` byte-unchanged, and every plan of
the earlier pass did the same. That assertion ends in this plan, and the authority
that replaces it is narrow to the point of being mechanical: **append only.** No
existing line is deleted, reordered or rewritten — not a disposition cell, not a
re-ratification field, not the completeness rule, not the anchor-drift warning.
The narrowed assertion in force is that the diff over this file contains no
removed line, and it is stated here rather than left implicit because an
append-only authority that is not checked is an edit authority.

**Why the governance record gets pointers and not menus (D-18).** The round's
contracted output is menus with marked defaults, and they live in the round's
consolidated document where they are attributed to the agents that produced them.
Copying them into the ruling record would put an unattributed menu inside the
document that records the owner's decision — which is exactly the shape of an
agent-authored option set, whatever its provenance. The record names where the
candidates are and states what a complete ruling must dispose of. It carries no
candidate.

**Neutrality standing.** Bar G runs over this plan file with its selector
byte-unchanged; this plan names no surface identifier and no candidate value. Bar
I, defined in this plan's `<verification>` block, governs the ruling record.
</planning_provenance>
