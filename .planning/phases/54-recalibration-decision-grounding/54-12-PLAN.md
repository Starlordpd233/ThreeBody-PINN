<objective>
Merge the two returned reports into the round's consolidated document, following
the join points the delivery note recorded before the round shipped, and land it
as a candidate for the owner's ratification.

Purpose: ERW Rule 12 names the consolidated document as the round's output and
Rule 11 requires the join points to be recorded in advance so that several
agents' reports merge deliberately rather than accidentally. This round's
delivery note recorded five, in a stated order, with a stated reason for each.
Two agents responded and they do not agree everywhere. The delivery note's own
instruction on that is explicit: disagreement between agents on a surface is a
finding, not noise, and it is carried into the decision sheet.

Output: `trainex-r3-resolution-doc.md` at the round root — the join record in the
delivery note's order, the cross-walk between each report's own decomposition and
the packet's surfaces, the consolidated candidate sheet attributed throughout
with an empty pick column, the merged inheritance and gaps, and the standing
paragraph that says what this document is and is not.

Per D-18 the menus live in this document, attributed to their authors, and never
in the governance record; per D-14 an agreement between the two reports carries
only the entitlement its claim class admits.
</objective>

<planning_provenance>
## Standing

`54-08` fixed the independence taxonomy. `54-09` and `54-10` gave every claim a
verdict and every cross-report agreement an entitlement. `54-11` adjudicated the
premises and recorded a re-frame determination together with an explicit
authorization literal for this plan. This plan runs only if that literal
authorizes it, which is why it is stated as this plan's precondition rather than
as a paragraph of prose.

**Tracer note.** This phase's tracer is `54-01`. `54-12` is an expansion plan on
that proven shape; no new tracer is emitted.

**The neutrality bars that governed the packet do not govern this document, and
the difference is deliberate.** The packet was required to mark no default, rank
no option and endorse nothing, because it was the instrument that framed a
question for the owner. This document is the round's contracted output, and a
build-design round's output contract requires exactly the opposite of the
packet's silence: menus, marked defaults, door classifications and a fill-in
sheet. The roadmap's own success criterion for this round names those elements as
what the round must return. Suppressing them here would discard the deliverable
the round was commissioned to produce.

**What replaces them is an attribution bar.** Every menu entry, every marked
default, every door classification and every ordering in this document belongs to
a returned report and carries a tag naming which one. The agent of record
transcribes and joins; it contributes no element of its own. That is the same
boundary SCOPE-34 draws, enforced by a different mechanism, because the element
being controlled is different: the packet's risk was the agent forming a
position, and this document's risk is the agent's join silently becoming one.

**The plan files remain under the packet's discipline.** Bar G runs over this
plan file with its selector byte-unchanged, so this plan names no surface
identifier and no candidate value anywhere, while the document it instructs
carries the agents' menus in full. A plan that named the values would put the
agent's voice where the agents' voices belong.

**Build the decision-row cross-walk first, and record it as a table.** The two
reports decompose the decision space differently: their decision-sheet rows do
not correspond one-to-one, and neither decomposition is the packet's. Silently
aligning them would lose whichever distinctions one report drew and the other did
not — and those distinctions are findings.

Columns: `Packet surface | Report one's row(s) | Report two's row(s) | Mapping kind | Note`.

Mapping kind is a value from a closed set:

- `ONE-TO-ONE` — both reports have a row addressing exactly this surface.
- `SPLIT` — one report divides this surface into more than one row. Record every
  sub-row, and record the distinction the split draws, because the split itself
  is that report's finding that the surface has an internal decision the packet
  did not surface.
- `MERGED` — one report addresses two packet surfaces in a single row. Record
  which, and record what the merge asserts about their coupling.
- `ADDITIONAL` — a report has a row that maps to no packet surface at all.
  Record it in full and mark it explicitly as lying outside the packet's
  enumeration. **Do not discard it and do not fold it into a neighbouring
  surface.** Where such a row would require widening the authorized scope, say so
  and cite the routed gap that carries the scope question; this document proposes
  no widening.
- `ABSENT` — a report has no row for this surface. Record the absence; a surface
  addressed by one report is single-source and the sheet says so.

**Then join the menus, one section per packet surface, in the packet's
transcription order** — repeating, once, that the order carries no precedence,
no priority and no sequencing implication, exactly as the ruling record states.

Each surface section carries:

1. **The capacity classification** from join three, restated at the head of the
   section so no reader reaches the menu without it.
2. **The joined option set.** Every option either report offers, with its
   attribution tag. Options both reports offer under different labels are **one**
   option carrying both labels and both attributions; the note records each
   report's own wording. Options only one report offers carry the single-source
   tag.
3. **Each report's marked default, attributed**, with the report's own reasoning
   in its own terms and its door classification. Where both reports mark a
   default and the marks differ, that is the finding the delivery note names:
   record both, record whether the capacity classification makes the
   disagreement resolvable or leaves it downstream of an unsettled dispute, and
   record it as a disagreement rather than resolving it. Where both mark the same
   default, record the entitlement the independence ledger assigned to that
   agreement — a matching default is not corroboration unless the class admits it.
4. **The verdicts.** Every claim the section leans on carries its verification
   verdict inline, and any claim marked unsettleable carries that marking.
5. **The premise markings** carried from join one, so an entry resting on a
   qualified or contradicted premise cannot be read without its qualification.
6. **The scope status.** Whether the surface is inside the authorized amendment
   set, and for any option that is not, an explicit marking that it lies outside
   it with the routed gap named.
7. **What the packet asked that neither report answered**, if anything, recorded
   as an unanswered framed question rather than omitted.

**Then build the consolidated candidate decision sheet.** Columns:
`Surface | Options, attributed | Each report's marked default, attributed | Door type, attributed | Trust status | Owner's pick`.

Rules the sheet must satisfy:

- **One row per enumerated packet surface, with none omitted**, including the
  surface the ruling record's pre-declared completeness rule names. That rule
  states that a ruling failing to dispose of it is incomplete, is routed back to
  the owner, and is never completed by an agent choice. A sheet missing that row
  would let a ruling be read off it that is incomplete by construction, so the
  row exists even where a report's own decomposition folded it into another.
- **Additional rows for every `ADDITIONAL` cross-walk entry**, in a separately
  headed group marked as lying outside the packet's enumeration, so a reader
  cannot mistake one for an enumerated surface.
- **Every cell that carries an option, a default, or a door type carries an
  attribution tag.** This is Bar H and it is asserted mechanically.
- **The trust status column** carries the capacity classification and, where
  applicable, the premise marking, so a row whose menus are untrusted says so on
  its own face. **Where both reports marked the same default on a surface, the
