Wrong call in either direction is recoverable but creates either redundant process artifacts (A) or an incomplete/uneven audit trail and ID→Filename map (B).

Choosing B unilaterally would set a process precedent by side-effect.
- **What the user needs to decide:** For PIPE-07 (and, as precedent, any future non-EVID-10 finding whose verdict already lives in a Phase 24 artifact):

  - **C.** Hybrid — author a thin pointer packet (`evidence/{name}.md`) that records the verdict + AC-ID and cites the Phase 24 artifacts as its dataset-evidence section without re-deriving them. Preserves packet-citation uniformity while avoiding duplication.
