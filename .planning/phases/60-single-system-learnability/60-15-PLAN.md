<objective>
If Gate G2 fired, run the pre-registered settings sweep: the four data-only settings of the
first-layer frequency on the recorded sweep system, one at a time, and record the curve and
which setting goes to the single confirmation run.

In plain language: when the network failed to fit two or more of the three orbits, this
checks whether one locked setting, rather than the network's design, is the bottleneck, by
trying four fixed alternatives on one failing orbit.

Purpose: FEAS-03's sweep (SPEC Requirement 6). Mutually exclusive with the G2-passed path:
this plan does nothing when G2 did not fire.

Output: four sweep arm records with weights and `evidence/g2/sweep-curve.json`, or a
"branch not taken" SUMMARY.
</objective>
