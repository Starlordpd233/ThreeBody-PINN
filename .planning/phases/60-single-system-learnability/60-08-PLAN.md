<objective>
Give real arms the frozen budget rule: a measured timing run sets each arm's step budget, the
ratified Adam schedule runs over exactly that budget, readings are scored at the frozen
cadence, the frozen still-improving test grants at most one extension as a second cosine,
divergence ends Adam at the last good state, and an undeterminable improvement rate goes to
the owner.

In plain language: before each experiment the tool measures how fast one training step runs,
gives the experiment exactly three hours of steps, checks its accuracy at regular points,
allows one doubling if it is still clearly improving, and never quietly retries anything the
rules say the owner must see.

Purpose: SPEC Requirements 4-5 (budget rule), D-01..D-05, GAP-60-03, GAP-60-04, GAP-60-05
(Adam wall), GAP-60-06. Physics arms, the cross-check and the complete record are Plan 60-11;
resume and signals are Plan 60-12.

Output: the budget driver and schedule in `learnability_run.py`, `tests/phase60/test_schedule.py`,
`tests/phase60/test_budget_driver.py`.
</objective>
