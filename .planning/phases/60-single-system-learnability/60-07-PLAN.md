<objective>
Build the launcher that keeps every long arm alive and accountable: it detaches from the
terminal and the agent session, runs one sequence of arms one at a time under caffeinate,
records every exit, and allows a resume only after a governed stop.

In plain language: generation 6 of the earlier training was lost because closing a session
killed it silently. This launcher runs the experiments in their own session, keeps the Mac
awake, writes down how each one ended, and refuses to restart anything that did not stop
cleanly.

Purpose: SCOPE-44 (detached, one at a time, generation-7 safeguards), SPEC Requirement 8
(an arm that cannot resume goes to the owner; no silent restart), CONTEXT discretion (launch
and resume use the generation-7 launcher's safeguards).

Output: `evidence/launch_phase60_arm.py` and `tests/phase60/test_launcher.py`.
</objective>
