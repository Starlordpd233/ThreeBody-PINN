## Dataset-Support Note

The dataset has two IC regimes with different mass characteristics:

- **Standard quasi-random:** masses are exactly `1.0` for all bodies (total mass exactly `3.0`). After canonical ordering, all three mass slots are identical. The normalized mass value is a single degenerate point.
  - `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:245` — "Standard ICs use exactly `1.0` for all bodies"
  - `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:323` — "Standard quasi-random: masses are exactly `1.0, 1.0, 1.0`"

- **Hierarchical:** masses vary (observed sample: min ≈ 0.105, median ≈ 0.897, max ≈ 2.377) but are normalized so total mass ≈ 3. After canonical ordering (descending mass), the three mass slots have different distributions, producing an asymmetric normalized range.
  - `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:245` — "hierarchical ICs vary but are normalized so total mass ≈ 3"
  - `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:324` — "Hierarchical: masses vary (observed sample: min ≈ 0.105, median ≈ 0.897, max ≈ 2.377) but are normalized so total mass ≈ 3"

The asymmetric occupancy under the v1.4 `tanh((m_i - mu_{m_i}) / (sigma_{m_i} + 10^-8))` formula is a direct consequence of the hierarchical mass distribution. No v1.4 numeric range is stated because the exact range depends on dataset-wide μ and σ per slot.
