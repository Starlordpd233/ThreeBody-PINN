<objective>
Close the ACC-06 grounding-backstop weakness (CR-05). The verdict-grounding test
must actually open and compare the cited receipt — parse typed YAML, validate a
real ISO date, load the receipt, and assert the verdict's gate, metrics, digests,
and allowed owner decision agree with it — so a mutated verdict citing the FAIL
receipt can no longer pass.

Purpose: the automated grounding test is the durable guarantee that the owner
verdict stays honest against its evidence; today it only string-matches and would
approve a `gate: PASS` / `verdict: go` document that still cites the FAIL receipt.
Output: a receipt-grounded validator plus receipt-mismatch, quoted-YAML, and
invalid-date regressions.
</objective>

## Artifacts this phase produces
- A rewritten `validate_verdict_grounding` that: parses frontmatter with `yaml.safe_load`; validates `owner_decision_date` with `datetime.date.fromisoformat`; resolves and loads the cited receipt (from `receipt_citation`, against a configurable base for testability); asserts frontmatter `gate` equals the receipt `verdict`, that the receipt `eps_h`/`scs` values and the checkpoint/config/splits digests appear in the verdict, and that the owner decision is allowed for that gate.
- New tests: receipt-mismatch (mutated gate/verdict/owner_decision vs. FAIL receipt → rejected), quoted-YAML (`gate: "FAIL"` still parsed correctly), invalid-date (an impossible calendar date rejected), and a skip-guarded real-receipt grounding test.
