```markdown
### 4.x Optional ALM Ablation (P2)

As a P2 ablation, an augmented Lagrangian method may be applied to scalar conservation
constraints only:

- **Constraints:** c_E = RMS_n(ΔH_n) (energy drift), c_{L,α} = RMS_n((L'_θ,α(u_n) − L_0,α') / L_scale)
  (angular momentum drift per component).
- **Never apply ALM to the dense ODE residual.**
- **Dual-variable updates:** Only between Adam epochs or between deterministic refinement passes.
  Never update dual variables during L-BFGS or inside a line search.
- **Logging:** Log constraint values before and after each dual-variable update.
- **Default:** ALM is OFF. The default loss is the penalty-term formulation in §3-§4 above.

This ablation is not part of the baseline training contract. It is documented for completeness
and for future experimentation.
```
