<objective>
Census EVERY author-flagged comment in any form/format across the three framework specs, verify all of them are preserved verbatim through the WS-A edits (0 lost, 0 semantically changed), and produce the `22-COMMENT-HANDOFF.md` handoff artifact that FUT-05 consumes for later adjudication. This is the REDUCED preserve-and-defer WS-B (GAP-22-01 RESOLVED → Defer): Phase 22 performs NO basis classification, NO adjudication, NO deletion. The ONLY permitted comment edit is a mechanical repair of a wrapper that itself breaks Markdown rendering, preserving the comment's text, meaning, and location (D-05).

Purpose: The author comments are the WS-A regression oracle AND a content asset that a dedicated post-v1.4 phase (FUT-05) will adjudicate. Phase 22's job is to guarantee they all survive the rendering cleanup intact and to hand them off cleanly. This satisfies ROADMAP Phase 22 SC 6 (census + verbatim preservation + FUT-05 handoff) and the G3 comment-preservation input.

Output: `22-COMMENT-HANDOFF.md` (FUT-05 inventory), `22-COMMENT-CENSUS-PRE.md` (committed before-snapshot for the preservation diff), and `22-COMMENT-CENSUS-POST.md` (committed after-snapshot).
</objective>
