<objective>
Discharge CONF-03: wire `ε_H = 1e-4 · S_H` into the loss assembly and remove the
§3.4-prohibited additive fallback floor. The defect is one line —
`pinn/training/controllers.py:482` currently scales ε by the per-anchor `abs(h0)` and
appends an additive floor the spec explicitly prohibits — but the plumbing does not
exist yet: `RunController.__init__` has no channel for a run-computed scalar
(Pitfall 5). This plan builds that channel as an explicit keyword-only required
constructor parameter, threads the 47-02-validated `S_H` through it, keeps the
per-anchor `abs(h0)` in the denominator where the spec puts it (D-20), preserves the
`_AnchorContext.eps_h` per-test override seam (D-21), and deletes the floor and its
rationalizing comment outright (D-19).

Loss magnitudes will move substantially — that is the frame becoming the locked one
and the scale becoming the specified one, not a signal to react to (D-26, SCOPE-29).

Purpose: the last Phase 47 requirement lands; Phase 48's loss-math conformance work
(CONF-04..07) inherits a spec-true `ε_H` path.
Output: the constructor channel, the corrected assembly line, the sweep of all twelve
test construction sites, the CONF-03 assembly-boundary tests, and the phase regression
gate.
</objective>
