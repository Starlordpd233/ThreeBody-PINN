## 1. Overview

v2.1 was the "separately governed investigation" that the v2.0 `adjust` verdict routed. The v2.0 milestone (the project's first build milestone) had shipped a working Stage-0 CTL-PINN training stack and then run the held-out physics-conservation evaluator exactly once — rendering an unequivocal **FAIL** (`ε_H = 237.21` against a `1e-5` reject threshold, across all 1,500 held-out trajectories). The owner signed `adjust` (2026-07-22): retain the mechanical slice, block full-platform expansion (PLAT-01..07), and investigate the failed gate under separate governance.

v2.1's job was **not** to fix the physics gate. Its job was two things:

1. **Diagnose** — from the evidence v2.0 already retained — how and plausibly why the Stage-0 model fails, producing a hypothesis-ranked, remedy-abstinent diagnosis report (`44-DIAGNOSIS.md`).
2. **Hand off** — assemble the trainex-r2 External Research Workflow (ERW) package so the owner's external research agents can adjudicate the remedy, returning a resolution doc that will serve as a candidate verdict source for the *next* milestone.

The milestone closes at package delivery. The remedy (recipe changes, new training, any new evaluation policy) belongs to the post-resolution-doc milestone — mirroring the v1.5/v1.6 package-first rhythm.

**What was delivered:** a read-only forensic diagnosis (Phase 44) and a complete, audited ERW research package (Phase 45), plus an unexpected bounded conformance audit that found the v2.0 Stage-0 pilot had diverged from the locked specs in five verified respects. All five gap questions were routed to the owner the same day; none remains open.

## 3. Phases

### Phase 44: Physics-Gate Forensics (completed 2026-07-23)

**Goal:** Characterize the failure from retained v2.0 artifacts — the held-out ε_H distribution, the training-time loss composition, and the seed band — and produce a hypothesis-ranked diagnosis that abstains from remedy selection.

**Deliverables (DIAG-01..04):**

- **DIAG-01** — Per-trajectory ε_H distribution over all 1,500 held-out trajectories (from preserved `metrics.json`, evaluator NOT re-run). **Finding: uniform, population-wide failure** — `min = 0.974 > 1e-5` (the best trajectory already misses by ~5 orders of magnitude), `max/median ≈ 106×`, `1500/1500` exceed individually. Neither `variant` nor `fidelity` separates the population.
- **DIAG-02** — Five-term loss decomposition per-step across smoke + 3 pilot seeds. **Finding: `loss_phys` dominates every warmup spike** (smoke 194/4.61e9, seed42 100/3.735e9 ≈ 3.7e9, shares 0.99998–0.99999), and dominates both raw AND λ-weighted cumulative (no flip). PHYS-05: `lambda_phys` first ≥ 1.0 at step 31 (all runs); `lambda_l ≡ 0.0` at all 300 steps.
- **DIAG-03** — Three-seed band characterization with Phase 38 overfit rung as machinery-integrity control. **Finding: 1/3 seeds worsened** (seed43 8.97e8→1.85e9), `relative_span = 3.82` (reproduces 43-CAL exactly). The Phase 38 single-trajectory overfit rung achieved `L0/Lf = 1097.61` (≥ 1e3) — the network CAN fit one trajectory; failure emerges at population scale.
- **DIAG-04** — `44-DIAGNOSIS.md` ranking 5 candidate causes (population-scale generalization **Strong**; loss-imbalance / optimization-warmup / normalization-scale **Moderate** tie; insufficient-budget **Weak**), each with supporting AND contradicting evidence, 23 [FACT] / 7 [HYP] tagged claims, explicit remedy abstinence, and GAP-44-01 routed to the owner.

**Verification:** 5/5 must-haves verified. All three diagnostic JSONs re-run byte-identical (SHA-256 unchanged); every number field-by-field cross-checked against the JSON; scope-boundary compliance (SCOPE-22..26) confirmed by tree cleanliness.
