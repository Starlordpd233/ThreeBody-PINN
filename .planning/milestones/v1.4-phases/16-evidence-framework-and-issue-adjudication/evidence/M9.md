## Dataset-Support Note

The dataset contains bound three-body systems. Bound gravitational systems have negative total energy (`E < 0`), so `tanh(E / E_scale)` maps into `[-1, 0)` — the positive side of the tanh range is unused. This is a property of the data-generation regime (bound systems selected by prescreening), not a property of the normalizer.

- `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:423` — Prescreening `ENERGY_ERROR_THRESHOLD = 0.01` (1% relative energy error). This is a dataset-process threshold, not a realized energy distribution range.
- `simulation_data/Documentation/README.md:442-443` — Prescreening rejects ICs with energy error > 1% or collisions (min separation < 0.01). These are dataset-process filters that produce a bound-system dataset.
