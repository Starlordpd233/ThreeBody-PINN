## What a clean verdict would and would not mean

`decide_verdict` rule 4 requires unanimous corrected-consistency across all 1651
trajectories. Three outcomes are live, and the plan treats all three as legitimate:

- **`VERDICT_TARGETS_ARE_RAW` (clean).** The resident surface holds raw-scale positions.
  46-07 ratifies the raw frame from the owner-recorded branch. GAP-46-01's reading (ii)
  is selected: the historical `evaluate.py` raw-time pairing was internally coherent, and
  the defect sits in the training targets.
- **`VERDICT_TARGETS_ARE_SCALED` (clean).** D-08 continuation; 46-07 ratifies the scaled frame.
- **`VERDICT_AMBIGUOUS` again.** Expected if `first_diverging_step` has moved to
  `rotation_gauge` or the `D` scaling — the probe walks the canonical pipeline in order and
  reports the *first* divergence, so removing two transforms can reveal the third. This is a
  measurement to route, not a failed repair, and **not** a licence to apply the next transform
  and try again. The owner decides whether a further minimum repair is authorized.
